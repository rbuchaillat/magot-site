Privacy policy

Updated November 11, 2026

In short: Magot collects no data. What you write down stays on your phone.

Who we are

Magot is published by Buchaillat Corp. For any question about this policy: remibuchaillat.pro@gmail.com.

The data you write down

The names, places and amounts you enter, their history and your settings (such as the reminder rhythm or the currency) are saved in a single file, on your phone. On iPhone, this file is encrypted by the system when your phone is locked.

The app works locally: what you write down is never sent over the internet. There’s no account, no server and no bank connection. So we have no access to your data, and we can’t read, sell or share it.

Widgets

To show your number, your pile and your goals on the home screen or the lock screen, the app saves a small summary (your total, the total of each category, the progress of your goals) in a space shared with its own widgets, on your phone. This summary never leaves your phone. On the lock screen, amounts stay hidden until your phone is unlocked.

Goals purchase

Goals are unlocked with a one-time purchase on the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Apple, under its own terms: we don’t receive your name, your email or your payment details. To know whether Goals are unlocked, the app asks the App Store, which answers yes or no. That’s the app’s only exchange with the internet, and it contains nothing of what you write down.

What we don’t collect

  • No personal data (name, email, phone, address).
  • No financial data: your amounts never leave your phone.
  • No usage statistics, no analytics, no ads, no trackers.
  • No third-party service built into the app.

Phone backups

Like other apps, Magot’s file is included in your phone’s backups if you turned them on (iCloud or computer on iPhone, Google backup on Android). These backups are managed by Apple or Google according to your settings, not by us.

Crash reports

If you agreed, in your phone’s settings, to share analytics with developers, Apple or Google may send us technical crash reports. They don’t contain what you wrote down in the app. You can turn this sharing off at any time in your phone’s settings.

Notifications

The check-up reminder is a notification scheduled by your phone itself, without a server. You can turn it off in the app’s Settings, or remove the permission in your phone’s settings.

Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your phone as long as you keep it there. To delete it: Settings, then “Erase everything” in the app, or delete the app. Since we don’t have any copy, there’s nothing to ask us to delete on our side.

Children

Magot collects no data, including from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its update date. If the app ever had to send data off your phone, this page would say so first.
