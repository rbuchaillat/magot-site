Frequently asked questions

Where is my data?

Only on your phone, in a single file. There’s no account and no server: we never see what you write down.

Does the app connect to my bank?

No. You write down each amount yourself. The check-up asks you, item by item, whether it’s the same or it moved.

If I change phones, do I lose everything?

Magot’s file is part of your phone’s backup (iCloud or computer). If you restore that backup on your new phone, your items come back. Without a backup they can’t be recovered: we don’t have any copy.

How does the reminder work?

In Settings, choose “Monthly” (the 1st of the month at 7 PM) or “Weekly” (Sundays at 7 PM). It’s a notification scheduled by your phone itself. If you just did a check-up, the next reminder is skipped.

What are Goals?

Set a goal for each category: it becomes a box that what you’ve got fills up. Enter what you put in each month, and Magot tells you when you’ll get there, and whether your deadline is realistic. Goals are unlocked with a one-time purchase, no subscription: the “Goals” button at the top of the home screen. Everything else in the app is free.

I changed phones and my Goals are locked

Your purchase is tied to your Apple Account, not to the phone. Sign in with the same account, open “Goals” and tap “Restore purchase”. You don’t pay again.

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of the home screen, tap “Edit” then “Add Widget”, and search for Magot. “Your stash” shows your number and your pile, in small, medium or large. “Goals” shows your boxes. On the lock screen, touch and hold it, then “Customize”: your amounts stay hidden until your phone is unlocked.

How do I change the currency?

Settings, then “Currency”. Magot starts with your country’s currency; only the symbol changes, your amounts stay the same.

How do I erase everything?

Settings, then “Erase everything”. Your items and their history are erased from your phone. Deleting the app erases everything too.

I just want to see what it looks like

Settings, then “Load the example”: the app fills up with made-up data so you can try everything.
